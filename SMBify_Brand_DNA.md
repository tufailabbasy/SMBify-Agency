# SMBify Agency - Brand DNA

## 1. Brand Identity
**Brand Name:** SMBify  
**Niche/Industry:** Local SEO for Home-Service Businesses (Plumbers, Electricians, HVAC, etc.)  
**Core Mission:** "We help home-service business owners dominate local search, rank at the top of the Google Map Pack, and secure a steady stream of phone calls for their trades."

## 2. Brand Tone & Voice
* **Authoritative & Expert:** We know what we are doing. We rely on data, proven strategies (Entity Stacking, Citation Building), and clear playbooks.
* **Direct & Actionable:** No fluff. We tell our clients exactly what works and what doesn't.
* **Modern & Premium:** Our agency feels high-end, trustworthy, and tech-forward. 
* **Conversational but Professional:** Friendly and approachable, yet strictly professional when it comes to business results.

## 3. Typography
We use modern, clean, and highly legible Google Fonts to convey a tech-forward agency feel.

* **Primary Font (Body Text):** `Inter` (sans-serif)
  * Usage: Paragraphs, descriptions, buttons, UI elements.
* **Secondary Font (Headings):** `Outfit` (sans-serif)
  * Usage: H1, H2, H3 headings, large display text, and numbers.

## 4. Brand Colors
Our color palette revolves around a sleek "Dark Mode" aesthetic contrasted with a vibrant "Lime" accent to signify growth, energy, and digital success.

### Primary Colors (Accents & CTAs)
* **Brand Lime (Primary Accent):** `#84CC16`
* **Brand Lime (Hover/Darker):** `#65A30D`
* **Brand Lime (Light/Glow):** `#BEF264`

### Dark Theme / Background Colors
* **Deep Space (Main Background):** `#070A13`
* **Midnight Blue (Secondary Background / Cards):** `#0D1220`
* **Slate Navy (Tertiary Background / Borders):** `#161E31`

### Text Colors (On Dark Backgrounds)
* **Text Primary (White/Off-white):** `#F9FAFB`
* **Text Secondary (Light Gray):** `#E5E7EB`
* **Text Tertiary (Muted Gray):** `#9CA3AF`

### UI Elements & Gradients
* **Hero Gradient:** Radial glow using `rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.15)` fading into dark blue `#070a13`.
* **CTA Gradient:** Linear gradient from `#84CC16` to `#65A30D`.
* **Glassmorphism:** We use semi-transparent backgrounds `rgba(13, 18, 32, 0.75)` with subtle white borders `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and heavy drop shadows to create depth.

## 5. Visual Aesthetic
* **Layouts:** Clean, grid-based, ample whitespace, 3-column structures.
* **Imagery:** Professional, high-quality agency and business owner imagery, often with dark overlays or glowing lime highlights.
* **UI Micro-interactions:** Smooth hover effects, glow animations on buttons, and floating cards.
