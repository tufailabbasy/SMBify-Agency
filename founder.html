Meet Muhammad Tufail Abbas

Driving Growth for Home Service Businesses

Muhammad Tufail Abbas is a dedicated local SEO veteran who has spent the last 7+ years helping local home-service businesses scale their operations. Starting with a simple mission—to simplify local search optimization for contractors and agencies—he has successfully managed over 945 projects and helped hundreds of plumbing, HVAC, and roofing companies double their lead flows.

After years of operating as a top-rated consultant, he founded SMBify to serve as a specialized, high-performing fulfillment engine that trades the usual agency fluff for real, transparent results.

The Journey

From KP to Global Scale

How a passion for local search led to building one of Pakistan's leading specialized Local SEO fulfillment agencies.

2018 — The Spark

Starting from a remote KP village with limited internet access, Tufail dedicated himself to self-learning digital skills, eventually specializing in WordPress backend structures and local map optimization.

2020 — Freelancing Breakthrough

Quickly gained recognition in the competitive global space, completing over 600 projects on Fiverr with a 99% client satisfaction rate, and achieving Upwork Top Rated status as a local SEO specialist.

2021 — Founding Tufi Digital

Formed Tufi Digital to scale operations. The firm specialized in local citation cleanup, business directories audit, and serving as a trusted white-label execution resource for Western marketing agencies.

2024 & Beyond — Launching SMBify

Founded SMBify as a specialized, A-Z local SEO platform built for trade niches (plumbing, roofing, HVAC, water damage, dumpster rental, tree services). The goal: handle 100% of the fulfillment headache for agencies and business owners.
