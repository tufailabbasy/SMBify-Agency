Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization for Fencing Contractors

Your Google Business Profile is the front door of your business on smartphones. To maintain top 3 Map Pack rankings:

  1. Set your primary business category accurately to match your core trade.
  2. Upload high-resolution geotagged project photos weekly directly from job sites.
  3. Maintain a steady review velocity by requesting customer reviews immediately upon job completion.
  4. Ensure exact NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all online directories.
