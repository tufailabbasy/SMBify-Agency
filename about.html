Who We Are

The Invisible Engine Behind Scalable Agencies

SMBify was born out of a simple realization: digital agencies want to focus on client relationships, sales, and strategy, but delivery bottlenecks and hiring stress constantly hold them back. We built the ultimate white-label local SEO fulfillment service to resolve this problem once and for all.

We work exclusively behind the scenes. Our team of certified SEO analysts, directory audit experts, and link outreach managers operates as your virtual in-house fulfillment team. You land the client; we handle the audits, citation cleanup, map configurations, schema deployment, and monthly reporting under your brand.

Our Journey

How We Scaled to Support Our Partners

From our roots as small campaign operators to managing local search listings for agencies and home service businesses worldwide.

2021 — The Spark

Founded by a small group of local SEO consultants. We initially specialized in manual citation building and cleaning up messy Google Map listings for local business directories.

2022 — Going 100% White-Label

After experiencing rapid client growth, we pivoted to support other digital marketing agencies. We automated onboarding, formalized strict NDA protections, and committed fully to unbranded (white-label) fulfillment.

2023 — Platform & Portal Launch

Developed our secure Local SEO intake portal and unbranded dashboard. This allowed our agency partners to submit customer data and track live local citation listings with 100% transparency.

2024 & Beyond — Focus & Expansion

Now actively managing campaigns for 5 agency partners and 50 local home service businesses across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

Our Philosophy

Standards That Protect & Build Trust

100% Quality & Manual Work

No automatic directory spin software, no generic templates, and no bulk fake accounts. Our team checks listing verification manually, ensuring indexation and permanent value.

Strict Confidentiality & NDA

We operate as a ghost partner. We do not contact your clients, reference your name, or leave code markers that disclose our partnership. Your clients stay yours forever.

How It Works

Fulfillment Made Effortless

From the moment you submit client details, our structured engine swings into action. Here is the pipeline:

01

Audit & Discovery

We run a thorough audit on your client's current NAP status, GBP settings, and existing citation duplicates.

02

Clean-Up Phase

Our operations team updates incorrect address details and removes duplicate profiles causing crawl errors.

03

NAP Optimization

We write metadata, configure JSON-LD Schema structures, and build geo-relevance maps for landing pages.

04

Listing Submission

Our listing managers manually submit to local business directories, search engines, and map databases.

05

Deliver & Report

We compile the logins, live links, and indexing updates, giving you a complete, unbranded PDF report ready to send.
