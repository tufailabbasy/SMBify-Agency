Who We Are

The Invisible Engine Behind Scalable Agencies

SMBify was born out of a simple realization: digital agencies want to focus on client relationships, sales, and strategy, but delivery bottlenecks and hiring stress constantly hold them back. We built the ultimate white-label local SEO fulfillment service to resolve this problem once and for all.

We work exclusively behind the scenes. Our team of certified SEO analysts, directory audit experts, and link outreach managers operates as your virtual in-house fulfillment team. You land the client; we handle the audits, citation cleanup, map configurations, schema deployment, and monthly reporting under your brand.

Our Journey

How We Scaled to Support 35+ Active Agencies

From our roots as small campaign operators to managing hundreds of concurrent local search listings worldwide.

2021 — The Spark

Founded by a small group of local SEO consultants. We initially specialized in manual citation building and cleaning up messy Google Map listings for local business directories.

2022 — Going 100% White-Label

After experiencing rapid client growth, we pivoted to support other digital marketing agencies. We automated onboarding, formalized strict NDA protections, and committed fully to unbranded (white-label) fulfillment.

2023 — Platform & Portal Launch

Developed our secure Local SEO intake portal and unbranded dashboard. This allowed our agency partners to submit customer data and track live local citation listings with 100% transparency.

2024 & Beyond — Global Expansion

Now actively managing campaigns for over 35+ agency partners across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. We continuously update our systems to reflect Google's Map Pack algorithm updates.

Got Questions?

Partner FAQs

Do you ever contact our agency's clients directly?

No, absolutely not. All processes are 100% white-label. We operate as your back-office team. We provide the client login credentials and report sheets to you, allowing you to handle the direct communication.

How long does campaign fulfillment take?

A standard local SEO package (citations, auditing, map config, and schema) is fully processed and submitted within 14 days of intake details validation. You will see tracking updates directly in the partner portal.

Do you work with non-agency businesses?

Yes. While our primary design is built for agencies looking for reseller rates and bulk options, small business owners and in-house marketing directors are welcome to use our standard monthly service tiers directly.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

None at all. All service plans are pay-as-you-go, paid on a month-to-month basis. You can cancel, downgrade, or upgrade your service tier for any of your client locations at any time with no penalties.
