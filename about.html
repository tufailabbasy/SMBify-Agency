Who We Are

The Engine Behind Home Service Local SEO Success

SMBify was built to solve a single, critical challenge: helping local trades and home-service businesses get found by local customers on Google Maps and search. We deliver specialized local SEO, citation cleanup, and map rankings that drive phone calls and book more jobs.

Our dedicated team of local SEO experts, directory coordinators, and technical analysts work systematically to manage your local search footprint. Whether you are a contractor looking to rank in your service areas, or an agency reselling our proven execution, we provide transparent results and zero contract traps.

Our Journey

How We Scaled to Support Our Clients

From our roots as small campaign operators to managing local search listings for home service businesses and partners worldwide.

2021 — The Spark

Founded by a small group of local SEO consultants. We initially specialized in manual citation building and cleaning up messy Google Map listings for local contractors.

2022 — Going 100% Home Services

Pivoted our strategy to focus on the home services niche. We optimized and standardized citation and mapping solutions specifically for the unique service area needs of contractors.

2023 — Platform & Portal Launch

Developed our secure Local SEO intake portal and client dashboard, allowing businesses and agency partners to submit data and track listings with 100% transparency.

2024 & Beyond — Scale & Dominance

Now actively managing campaigns for 5 agency partners and 50 local home service businesses across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

Our Philosophy

Standards That Protect & Build Trust

100% Quality & Manual Work

No automatic directory spin software, no generic templates, and no bulk fake accounts. Our team checks listing verification manually, ensuring indexation and permanent value.

Strict Confidentiality & NDA

We operate as a ghost partner. We do not contact your clients, reference your name, or leave code markers that disclose our partnership. Your clients stay yours forever.

Transparent Data Tracking

Every submission is verified with login details and live page links. You can export complete lists in CSV format and run ranking reports directly for client reviews.

How It Works

Fulfillment Made Effortless

01

Audit & Discovery

02
