The Ultimate Local SEO Dictionary for Contractors

Don't let agency jargon confuse you. Our complete glossary breaks down the exact terms, concepts, and strategies we use at SMBify to dominate local search for home service businesses.

Algorithm

A complex set of rules and formulas search engines use to determine the relevance and ranking of webpages for specific queries.

Alt Text

Alternative text applied to images to describe them for visually impaired users and to help search engines understand the image content.

Anchor Text

The visible, clickable text in a hyperlink. Using relevant keywords in anchor text can help improve the linked page's ranking.

Backlink

A link from one external website pointing to your website. Backlinks are viewed as 'votes of confidence' by search engines.

Black Hat SEO

Aggressive and unethical SEO tactics (like keyword stuffing or buying links) that violate search engine guidelines and risk penalties.

Bounce Rate

The percentage of visitors who land on a webpage and leave (bounce) without interacting with the page or visiting other pages.

Canonical Tag

An HTML element (rel="canonical") that tells search engines which version of a URL is the master copy, preventing duplicate content issues.

Citation

Any online mention of a local business's Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP). Vital for local search rankings.

Citation Velocity

The speed and frequency at which new business listings are created. A natural, consistent velocity is best.

Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The ratio of users who click on a specific link to the number of total users who view a page, email, or advertisement (impressions).

Content Silo

A method of grouping related content together in a website's architecture to establish strong topical authority for a specific subject.

Core Web Vitals

A set of specific factors that Google considers important in a webpage's overall user experience (loading, interactivity, visual stability).

Crawlability

How easily search engine bots (like Googlebot) can discover and navigate the pages and links on a website.

Data Aggregators

Major database companies (Factual, Localeze, Foursquare, Data Axle) that compile business data and distribute it to hundreds of directories.

Domain Authority (DA)

A search engine ranking score developed by Moz that predicts how well a website will rank on search engine result pages.

Duplicate Content

Substantive blocks of content within or across domains that either completely match other content or are appreciably similar.

E-E-A-T

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. A framework Google uses to assess the quality of content and creators.

Featured Snippet

A summarized answer to a search query displayed at the very top of Google’s search results, extracted from a webpage.

Geo-Targeting

The practice of delivering localized content, landing pages, or advertisements to a user based on their geographic location.

Geofencing

Using GPS or RFID technology to create a virtual geographic boundary, enabling targeted marketing when devices enter the area.

Google Business Profile (GBP)

A free tool allowing business owners to manage how their local business appears across Google Search and Google Maps.

Google Search Console (GSC)

A free service offered by Google that helps monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot a site's presence in Google Search results.

H1 Tag

The primary HTML heading of a webpage. It should clearly describe the page's main topic and typically includes the primary keyword.

Hyperlocal SEO

An advanced local search strategy focused on targeting customers in highly specific geographic areas, like neighborhoods or zip codes.

Indexability

A search engine's ability to analyze a webpage and add it to its database (index) so it can be displayed in search results.

Keyword Cannibalization

When multiple pages on the same website compete for the exact same search query, diluting ranking power.

Keyword Density

The percentage of times a keyword or phrase appears on a web page compared to the total number of words on the page.

Keyword Stuffing

The spammy tactic of overloading a page (or GBP name) with keywords to manipulate rankings, which violates Google's guidelines.

Link Juice

An SEO term referring to the value or equity passed from one page or site to another via a hyperlink.

Local Link Building

The process of acquiring backlinks from locally relevant websites in your specific geographic area (e.g., local news, chamber of commerce).

Local Map Pack (3-Pack)

The prominent block of three local business listings that appears at the top of Google search results for local queries.

Long-Tail Keywords

Highly specific, longer search phrases that typically have lower search volume but much higher conversion intent.

Meta Description

An HTML attribute providing a brief summary of a webpage. It often appears as the snippet of text below the title in search results.

NAP Consistency

Ensuring your business Name, Address, and Phone number are exactly the same across your website and all online directories.

Niche Edits

A link-building strategy where a link to your site is inserted into an existing, aged article on another website.

Off-Page SEO

Actions taken outside of your own website to impact your rankings (e.g., link building, social media marketing, local citations).

On-Page SEO

The practice of optimizing individual web pages (content, HTML, images) in order to rank higher and earn more relevant traffic.

Orphan Page

A webpage that has no internal links pointing to it, making it extremely difficult for users and crawlers to find.

PBN (Private Blog Network)

A network of websites created primarily to build links to a single target website. Highly risky and penalized by Google.

Proximity

A major ranking factor in local search indicating how close the user physically is to the business's address.

RankBrain

A component of Google's core algorithm which uses machine learning to determine the most relevant results to search engine queries.

Review Velocity

The consistent rate at which a business acquires new online reviews. Natural velocity builds high trust with Google.

Robots.txt

A text file located in the root directory of a website that tells search engine crawlers which pages or files they can or can't request.

Schema Markup (JSON-LD)

Structured data code added to a website to help search engines understand the context of the content (e.g., reviews, local business info).

Search Intent

The primary goal a user has when typing a query into a search engine (e.g., informational, navigational, transactional).

Service Area Business (SAB)

A business that visits or delivers to customers (like plumbers or roofers) but doesn't serve them at a storefront. SABs hide their addresses.

Sitemap (XML)

A file where you provide information about the pages, videos, and other files on your site, and the relationships between them.

Technical SEO

Website and server optimizations that help search engine spiders crawl and index your site more effectively.

Title Tag

An HTML element that specifies the title of a web page. It is displayed as the clickable headline in search engine results.

Topical Authority

A measure of the depth of expertise a website has on a specific subject, achieved by covering a topic comprehensively through content silos.

White Hat SEO

Ethical SEO practices that improve search performance while strictly following search engine rules and policies.

White-Label SEO

When an agency (like SMBify) fulfills SEO services entirely under the brand name of another marketing agency.
