The Ultimate Local SEO Dictionary for Contractors

Don't let agency jargon confuse you. Our complete glossary breaks down the exact terms, concepts, and strategies we use at SMBify to dominate local search for home service businesses.

Citation Velocity

The speed and frequency at which new business listings (citations) are created across directories over a specific period. A natural, consistent velocity is safer and more effective than building hundreds of citations in a single day.

Data Aggregators

Massive database companies (such as Factual, Neustar Localeze, Foursquare, and Data Axle) that compile business data and distribute it to hundreds of smaller directories, search engines, and mapping apps. Submitting to aggregators is crucial for local SEO.

Geo-Targeting

The practice of delivering localized content, landing pages, or advertisements to a user based on their geographic location. E.g., creating a dedicated "Plumber in Dallas, TX" page for a Texas-based contractor.

Google Business Profile (GBP)

Formerly known as Google My Business (GMB). A free tool that allows business owners to manage how their local business appears across Google Search and Google Maps. It is the single most important element in local SEO.

Hyperlocal SEO

An advanced local search strategy focused on targeting customers in highly specific geographic areas, such as a specific neighborhood, block, or zip code, rather than just an entire city.

Keyword Stuffing (GBP)

The spammy (and risky) tactic of adding unnecessary keywords into a Google Business Profile business name (e.g., "Joe's Plumbing - Best Dallas Plumber"). This violates Google's guidelines and often leads to listing suspension.

Local Link Building

The process of acquiring backlinks from locally relevant websites in your specific geographic area, such as local news outlets, neighborhood blogs, regional chambers of commerce, or local event sponsorships.

Local Map Pack (3-Pack)

The prominent block of three local business listings (and a map) that appears at the top of Google search results for queries with local intent (e.g., "roofers near me"). Ranking here is the primary goal of Local SEO.

NAP Consistency

Stands for Name, Address, and Phone number. Having exactly matching NAP information across your website, Google Business Profile, and all online directories is a critical trust factor for search engines.

Proximity to Searcher

A major algorithmic ranking factor in local search indicating how close the user physically is to the business's address. Businesses closer to the person searching generally have a higher chance of showing up in the Map Pack.

Review Velocity

The speed and consistent rate at which a business acquires new online reviews. A steady stream of 2-3 reviews per month is seen by Google as much more natural and authoritative than getting 50 reviews in one day and zero for a year.

Service Area Business (SAB)

A business model where the contractor visits or delivers to customers directly but doesn't serve customers at its own physical storefront (e.g., plumbers, HVAC, roofers). SABs hide their address on Google Business Profiles.
