Inside the Local Authority Engine

Our Core Local SEO Deliverables

We don't just do checklist-based SEO. We deliver a complete authority-building system that Google trusts, making it easy for local clients to find you.

Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization

We fully optimize your business listing. This includes category alignment, service menu setup, keyword-rich business description, high-quality geo-tagged photos, custom Q&As, and weekly Google posts to show active trust signals.

Manual Citation Building (NAP Consistency)

Consistent Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) across directories is crucial. We clean up incorrect listings, remove duplicates, and manually build local citation profiles on top directories (Yelp, YellowPages, Bing, Apple Maps, Foursquare, etc.) to establish local presence.

Learn More →

Local Link Building & Map Embeds

We build localized backlinks from geo-relevant blogs, community sites, and business directories. Additionally, we create customized local map embeds on Web 2.0 properties, sending coordinates and proximity signals back to Google Maps.

On-Page SEO & Schema Markup

We configure advanced LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema markup on your website. This tells search engines exactly where your business is, what areas you serve, and your business hours. We also optimize headings, title tags, and meta text for local keywords.

Review Acquisition & Reputation Strategy

Reviews are a top-3 ranking factor on Google Maps. We provide a custom review acquisition strategy, QR code review cards, and templates to request and reply to reviews properly. We also set up automated review-checking alerts.

Ranking & Keyword Position Reporting

We track keyword performance transparently. You get a monthly rank tracking report showing keyword movements on Google Maps and Google Search, GBP search queries and insights (calls, direction requests), and citation indexing status.
