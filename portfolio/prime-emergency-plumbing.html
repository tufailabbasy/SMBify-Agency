Background

This client approached us with no existing web presence in a highly competitive emergency plumbing niche where most established local competitors had years of domain history. Rather than optimizing an existing platform, the project started completely from zero.

Since the site was structured as a lead generation CPC website, every technical, structural, and topical authority decision was made from day one with search intent and traffic monetization in mind.

What We Did

We built a custom website from scratch, creating a silo structure designed around localized service and location pages rather than using a generic, shallow template. Developed an off-page strategy including cloud authority stacks and guest posting to build trust without relying on spammy, low-quality directories.

We also implemented clean semantic markup and entity-based schema structures, ensuring search engines and AI models understand the services offered and the exact geographic areas of operation.

Why This Matters

Most local SEO work stops at ranking in traditional search engines. This project was specifically engineered to also surface in AI search engines, including ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode, which capture a growing share of local, high-intent traffic.

This proactive AI optimization serves as a leading indicator, driving high-converting actions before rankings fully mature in classic search.

Financial Performance

The website has officially commenced lead capture and monetization operations. Thanks to high rankings and targeted traffic, the site is now earning consistently and has successfully generated a remarkable return of 350% of the initial investment (350% ROI) for the client.
