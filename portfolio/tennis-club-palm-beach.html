Background

The Tennis Club of Palm Beach is a premium tennis facility offering state-of-the-art courts, private coaching, and junior program signups in Palm Beach, Florida. Despite their world-class facilities and experienced instructors, the club struggled to capture organic local visibility. They were buried under older, established tennis clubs and regional country clubs in Google Maps searches.

They approached SMBify to optimize their local search presence, target high-volume sport keywords, and drive booking leads directly through their Google Business Profile.

What We Did

We initiated a multi-layered local SEO campaign. We re-optimized their Google Business Profile by mapping primary and secondary categories ('Tennis club', 'Tennis court', 'Tennis instructor'), completed the service list, and added localized, geo-tagged photos of the courts and coaching sessions.

We cleaned up duplicate citation records and built niche local Florida directories. To establish entity authority, we implemented advanced cloud stack structures and injected structured schema markups containing geographical coordinates and service-area info to signal strong local relevance.

Why This Matters

Tennis bookings and junior coaching enrollments are highly localized, high-intent transactions. When parents or sports enthusiasts search for "tennis club near me" or "tennis instructor palm beach", they want to find, evaluate, and contact a club immediately.

By ranking in the Rank 1 position on Google Maps, the Tennis Club of Palm Beach directly intercepts this immediate booking intent, leading to a steady flow of lessons, bookings, and court bookings without relying on high paid advertising costs.

Results & Growth

The optimization yielded remarkable local visibility. Local Falcon grid scans registered a **77% Local Business Visibility score** across their target area, claiming the **Rank 1 position** for their core target keyword. This local authority resulted in a massive surge in direct calls and coaching signup inquiries.
