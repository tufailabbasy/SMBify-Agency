Background

Hello Gorgeous is a premier beauty and hair salon located in Naples, Florida. Despite providing top-tier salon, styling, and aesthetic services, the business suffered from a lack of local online visibility. Their Google Business Profile (GBP) was not optimized, and their website struggled to appear for key salon-related search terms in their target geographic radius.

They approached us to run a combined local search campaign: optimize their Google Business Profile and handle technical website SEO adjustments to capture active local clients.

What We Did

Conducted local competitor auditing to target high-value services. Re-optimized their Google Business Profile: updated core categories, expanded service details, added geo-tagged photos, and implemented a proactive customer review strategy.

On the website, we resolved core technical SEO errors, injected structured local schema, optimized header hierarchy, and improved mobile viewport loading speed to ensure seamless appointment bookings.

Why This Matters

Local service searches in the beauty industry are highly geographic and driven by immediate booking intent. When a client searches for "hair salon naples" or "beauty salon in Naples FL", they are looking to schedule an appointment.

Appearing in the top positions of the Google Map Pack and local search results ensures the salon directly intercepts these high-converting leads, outranking competitors and building long-term local brand equity.

Results & Growth

The campaign delivered significant results. The salon reached 80% map visibility in a 5km radius, securing the Rank 1 spot for "hair salon naples" and Rank 2 for "beauty salon naples". Organic search performance followed, driving 150 high-intent clicks and 23.6K search impressions in just 28 days.
