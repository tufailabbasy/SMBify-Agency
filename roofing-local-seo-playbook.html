Roof replacement is one of the highest ticket trades in residential contracting. With Google Local Service Ads costing upwards of $150 to $250 per lead, building a dominant organic Local SEO engine is the ultimate competitive advantage for roofing contractors.

🔴 Storm Chasing Doorway Page Penalty

Creating temporary, thin doorway pages during a storm without building permanent localized authority results in Google de-indexing those pages right when homeowners are searching for inspectors.
