Why Automated APIs are a Trap

Many software aggregators use temporary API feeds (like Yext, Moz Local, or Advice Local) to push your business data. While fast, they come with a major catch:

  • The Rent-to-Own Trap: If you stop paying their annual software subscriptions, they disconnect the API, and your listings immediately revert or disappear.
  • Locked Data: You never receive direct logins or passwords to edit your directories manually.
  • Ignored Duplicates: API networks push new listings but rarely find or clean up legacy incorrect listings.
